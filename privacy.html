Privacy Policy & Support — last updated June 19, 2026

Support

Need help? Email bharv14@gmail.com and we'll get back to you.

What we collect

  • Account: the email you sign up with.
  • Your logs: the meals, weights, workouts, and optional sleep you record. These are saved to your account.
  • Chat messages: what you type to the coach. These are not stored on our servers — they are sent to the AI provider only to generate a reply, then discarded.

We do not collect contacts, location, photos, or tracking identifiers.

How we use it

  • To save your progress and show it back to you across sessions and devices.
  • To generate coaching replies. Your chat message, plus a short summary of your day's fitness data (including your latest weight, and whether you ate, lifted, slept, or weighed), is sent to our AI provider (Google Gemini) to produce a response. This is used only to answer you — never to train models, and never sold to anyone.

Storage & isolation

Data is stored securely with Supabase. Each account's data is isolated; you can only access your own records.

Deleting your data

Delete your account and all data anytime from Settings → Delete account in the app. Immediate and irreversible.

Children & medical

Not directed to children under 13. Provides general fitness/motivation content, not medical advice — consult a professional before changing your diet or exercise.
