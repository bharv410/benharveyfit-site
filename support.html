Quick help

  • How do I log something? Tap a pill (Ate / Lift / Weigh) or just type it in the chat — e.g. "ate chicken and rice" or "186". The AI coach logs it and replies.
  • How do I log my weight? Type the number (like "186") in the chat, or tap the Weigh tile and enter it.
  • I don't want to track sleep. Open Settings (gear, top-right) → turn off "Track Sleep". Your score won't count it.
  • How do I delete my account? Settings → Delete account. This permanently removes your account and all your data.
  • The coach didn't reply. Usually a network hiccup — your logs still save. Try again in a moment.
